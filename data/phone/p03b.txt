L3-03   Project scope   项目范围 
①Project scope is the work that must be performed to deliver a product, service or result with the specified features. ② It is documented in the project scope 
statement and then decomposed into work packages in the WBS.  
项目范围写在项目范围说明书里，再在 WBS 中分解成工作包。例如交付一个移动 App 需要设计、编码、集成、测试、培训和上线等工作，这些都必须出现在项目范围
中。 
L3-04   Requirement   需求 
①A requirement is a condition or capability that must be met by the project; a good requirement is measurable. ② Requirements are collected through interviews, 
workshops, prototypes, observation and document analysis, and then analysed, prioritised and approved.  
需求通过访谈、工作坊、原型、观察和文档分析收集，然后分析、排优先级并审批。例如「系统 95% 的请求必须在 2 秒内响应」是可用的需求，而「系统要快」不是，
因为无法测试。 
L3-05   Scope management plan   范围管理计划 
①The scope management plan documents how project and product scope will be defined, validated and controlled. ② It defines the processes, roles, templates and 
the change procedure that will be used to define, validate and control scope throughout the project.  
它规定整个项目期间用于定义、核实和控制范围的过程、角色、模板和变更程序。例如其中会写明 WBS 必须在执行前得到发起人批准、任何新功能都必须走正式变更
申请。 
L3-06   Project charter   项目章程 
①The project charter is the document issued by the sponsor that formally authorises the project and gives the project manager authority to apply resources. ② It 
states the project purpose, high-level scope, key milestones, budget, main risks, the sponsor and the authority of the project manager, and it is issued before detailed 
planning begins.  
它写明项目目的、高层级范围、关键里程碑、预算、主要风险、发起人以及 PM 的权限，并在详细计划之前签发。例如银行批准移动支付项目时，章程会指定发起人和
项目经理，并授权支出第一笔预算。 
L3-07   Project scope statement   项目范围说明书 
①The project scope statement describes the project in detail: deliverables, boundaries, acceptance criteria and exclusions. ② It contains the product scope 
description, the deliverables, the acceptance criteria, the project exclusions, the constraints and the assumptions.  
它包含产品范围描述、可交付成果、验收标准、项目排除项、约束和假设。例如其中写明 App 覆盖 iOS 与安卓，但网页版明确不在本项目范围内。 
L3-08   WBS   工作分解结构 
①A WBS is a deliverable-oriented hierarchical decomposition of the total scope of work. ② It is built level by level, from the whole project to major deliverables, then 
to sub-deliverables and finally to work packages, and every level must contain all the work of the level above.  
WBS 逐层构建：从整个项目到主要可交付成果，再到子交付物，最后到工作包，每一层必须包含上一层全部工作。例如建房项目可分解为设计、结构、机电、装修、
移交。 
L3-09   Work package   工作包 
①A work package is the lowest level of the WBS, where cost and duration can be estimated and work can be assigned. ② It is small enough to be estimated in cost 
and duration and to be assigned to a single owner, but not so small that controlling it costs more than doing it.  
它小到可以估算成本和工期、可以交给唯一责任人，但又不能小到管理成本超过做事成本。例如「完成二层电气布线」可以是一个工作包，工期 5 天、预算 8000 美元。 
L3-10   Decomposition   分解 
①Decomposition is the technique of subdividing project scope and deliverables into smaller, more manageable components. ② It can be done top-down from the 
total scope or bottom-up by grouping detailed tasks, and the team should participate because they know the work best.  
分解可以自上而下从总范围开始，也可以自下而上把细任务归组，并且应让团队参与，因为他们最懂工作。例如「测试」可分解为单元测试、集成测试、用户验收测
试。 
L3-11   100% rule   100% 规则 
①The 100% rule states that the WBS must include 100% of the work defined by the project scope and nothing more. ② It applies at every level of the WBS: the sum of 
the child elements must equal 100% of the parent element, no more and no less.  
它在 WBS 每一层都适用：子元素之和必须正好等于父元素的 100%，不能多也不能少。例如培训没写进 WBS，就不会被估算、不会排期、不会配资源，最后就是不做。 
L3-12   WBS dictionary   WBS 词典 
①The WBS dictionary describes each WBS component: its scope, deliverables, acceptance criteria, cost and responsible person. ② It records the code of accounts, the 
description of the work, the deliverables, the acceptance criteria, the cost, the duration, the responsible person and the dependencies of each WBS component.  
它记录每个 WBS 元素的账目编码、工作描述、可交付成果、验收标准、成本、工期、责任人和依赖关系。例如「二层电气布线」这个工作包，词典会列出材料、要遵
循的电气标准和负责电工的姓名。 
L3-13   Scope baseline   范围基准 
①The scope baseline is the approved project scope statement plus the WBS and the WBS dictionary. ② It is approved by the sponsor and can only change through 
integrated change control, which is why it gives a stable reference for measuring performance.  
它由发起人批准，只能通过整体变更控制修改，因此能为绩效度量提供稳定参照。例如批准一个新功能时，范围说明书、WBS 和 WBS 词典必须一起更新，基准才保持
一致。 
L3-14   OBS   组织分解结构 
①The OBS is an organisational breakdown structure that assigns work packages to departments and owners. ② It breaks the organisation down into departments, 
units and teams, and each work package is mapped to one organisational unit.  
它把组织分解为部门、单元和团队，每个工作包映射到一个组织单元。例如 WBS 中的「测试」在 OBS 中对到质量部，「施工」对到工程部。 
L3-15   RAM / RACI   责任分配矩阵 
①A responsibility assignment matrix links work packages to people; RACI stands for responsible, accountable, consult and inform. ② R means responsible, the person 
who does the work; A means accountable, the one person who owns the result and approves it; C means consulted, people who give input; I means informed, people 
who are kept up to date.  
R 是执行者（真正干活的人），A 是问责者（唯一对结果负责并批准的人），C 是被咨询者（提供意见），I 是被告知者（只需知会）。例如某个工作包：工程师是 R，
PM 是 A，质量部是 C，发起人是 I。
