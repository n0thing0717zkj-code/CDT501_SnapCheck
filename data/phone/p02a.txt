L2-03   Talent triangle   人才三角 
①The PMI talent triangle defines three skill areas: ways of working, power skills and business acumen. ② Ways of working are the technical skills such as scheduling, 
budgeting and risk analysis; power skills are leadership, communication, negotiation and conflict resolution; business acumen is strategy, finance and industry 
knowledge.  
工作方式是技术技能（排程、预算、风险分析）；人际技能是领导力、沟通、谈判、冲突解决；商业敏锐度是战略、财务与行业知识。例如会画完美甘特图却搞不定
强势发起人的 PM，技术强但人际弱。 
L2-04   Greenfield project   新建场地项目 
①A greenfield project is built on a new site, starting from scratch with few existing constraints. ② The team can choose the site, the technology and the design freely, 
and there are few legacy systems or existing users to integrate with.  
团队可以自由选择场地、技术和设计，几乎没有旧系统或既有用户需要整合。例如在空地上建新工厂、在新厂房里上线全新产品线，都是新建场地项目。 
L2-05   Brownfield project   既有场地项目 
①A brownfield project is built on an existing site, so it faces many constraints and interfaces. ② The team must work around existing buildings, utilities, systems and 
users, so interfaces and hidden constraints are the main source of risk.  
团队必须绕开既有建筑、管线、系统和用户，接口与隐藏约束是主要风险来源。例如老工厂不停产改造、银行核心系统在不停业的情况下替换，都是既有场地项目。 
L2-06   Methodology   方法论 
①A methodology is a system of practices, techniques, procedures and rules used by those who work in a discipline. ② It is prescriptive: it tells the team what to do, in 
what order, with which documents and templates, and how each stage should be approved.  
方法论是规定性的：告诉团队做什么、按什么顺序、用什么文档模板、每个阶段如何审批。例如 PRINCE2、或企业自有的阶段关卡流程，都是方法论，因为它详细规定
了角色、产出和决策点。 
L2-07   Framework   框架 
①A framework is a flexible structure of principles and guidelines that can be adapted to the project. ② It provides principles, roles and processes that the team must 
adapt to the specific project, so two projects can use the same framework in very different ways.  
框架只给原则、角色和流程，团队必须根据具体项目做剪裁，所以同一框架在两个项目里用法可以完全不同。例如 PMBOK 指南和 Scrum 都是框架：描述良好实践长什
么样，细节留给团队裁剪。 
L2-08   Critical chain project management   关键链项目管理 
①Critical chain project management schedules with resource buffers and protects the longest resource-constrained path. ② It removes the safety time hidden in each 
activity, aggregates that time into buffers at the end of the chain, and controls the project by monitoring how fast the buffers are consumed.  
它把每个活动里藏的安全时间抽出来，集中成链条末端的缓冲，并通过监控缓冲消耗速度来控制项目。例如五名工程师共用一台测试设备时，关键链按受资源约束的
顺序排，并用汇入缓冲保护它。 
L2-09   Strategic management   战略管理 
①Strategic management is the science of formulating, implementing and evaluating cross-functional decisions to achieve organisational objectives. ② It links the 
mission and objectives of the organisation to strategies, and then to programs and projects that actually deliver the strategy.  
它把组织的使命与目标连到战略，再连到真正落地战略的项目集和项目。例如零售商决定进入线上市场，这个战略就通过由 IT、物流、营销多个项目组成的电商项目
集来实现。 
L2-10   TOWS matrix   TOWS 矩阵 
①The TOWS matrix pairs external threats and opportunities with internal weaknesses and strengths to generate SO, ST, WO and WT strategies. ② SO strategies use 
internal strengths to exploit external opportunities, WO strategies overcome internal weaknesses to capture opportunities, ST strategies use strengths to avoid 
external threats, and WT strategies minimise weaknesses and avoid threats.  
SO 用优势抓机会，WO 补劣势抓机会，ST 用优势避威胁，WT 减少劣势并避开威胁。例如研发能力强的企业（优势）借此进入增长市场（机会），就是 SO 战略。 
L2-11   Stakeholder   干系人 
①Stakeholders are all individuals or groups who have an active stake in the project and can potentially impact its outcome. ② Stakeholders include the sponsor, the 
customer, the users, the project team, functional managers, suppliers, the government and even the public, and they can be internal or external to the organisation.  
干系人包括发起人、客户、用户、项目团队、职能经理、供应商、政府甚至公众，可以是组织内部或外部的。例如工地旁的居民也是干系人：噪音和交通影响他们，
而他们的投诉可能让工程停工。 
L2-12   Stakeholder register   干系人登记册 
①A stakeholder register records each stakeholder's role, expectations, influence and management strategy. ② It is a living document that records contact details, role, 
expectations, influence, power/interest rating, current engagement level and the chosen management strategy.  
它是活文档：记录联系方式、角色、期望、影响力、权力/利益评级、当前参与程度和所选管理策略。例如登记册里发起人是高权力高利益、「重点管理」，供应商是
「保持告知」。 
L2-13   Power/interest grid   权力-利益矩阵 
①The power/interest grid classifies stakeholders by power and interest: manage closely, keep satisfied, keep informed or monitor. ② High power and high interest 
stakeholders must be managed closely, high power and low interest stakeholders must be kept satisfied, low power and high interest stakeholders must be kept 
informed, and low power and low interest stakeholders only need monitoring.  
高权力高利益要重点管理，高权力低利益要保持满意，低权力高利益要保持告知，低权力低利益只需监控。例如监管机构权力高但日常兴趣低，PM 用定期合规报告让
它满意，而不是天天汇报。 
L2-14   Cooperation-threat matrix   合作-威胁矩阵 
①The cooperation/threat matrix classifies stakeholders by their willingness to cooperate and their potential to threaten the project. ② Stakeholders range from 
supportive, who cooperate and pose little threat, to marginal, to non-supportive, to adversarial, who actively oppose the project and have the power to damage it.  
干系人从支持型（合作、威胁小）到边缘型、非支持型，再到对抗型（公开反对且有破坏力）。例如一个既可能支持项目、又可能把信息泄露给竞争对手的供应商，
就落在高合作、高威胁象限。 
L2-15   Communication plan   沟通计划 
①A communication plan defines who needs what information, when, in what form and from whom. ② It specifies the sender, the receiver, the message content, the 
medium, the frequency and the feedback mechanism for each audience, and it becomes part of the project management plan.  
它规定每个受众的发送者、接收者、内容、媒介、频率和反馈方式，并纳入项目管理计划。例如发起人每周五收一页状态报告，团队每天 15 分钟站会，客户每轮迭代
看一次演示。
